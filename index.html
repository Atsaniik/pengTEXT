International Academic Recognition

2nd Place – IFITT Awards 2024

Recognized by the International Federation for IT and Travel & Tourism for Peng Yang's research excellence: Constructing A Holistic Content Analytical Framework.

This study proposes a holistic content analytical framework to examine online user-generated content (OTC) across multiple platforms, using the destination of Finland as a case example. The proposed framework integrates textual, visual, and temporospatial data analyses. Specifically, it employs multilingual topic modeling and network analysis for textual reviews, object detection and optical character recognition for visual imagery, and time-series mapping for visitor movement. By synthesizing these multi-modal data streams, the framework evaluates destination image perceptions, prominent tourist attractions, and visitor retention patterns.

Methodological Innovation

KPTopic: A Topic Generator Built on the Semantic Network of Key Parts of Speech

Peng A. Yang, Juho Pesonen, Heli Hallikainen, and Andrei Kirilenko

Information and Communication Technologies in Tourism (2026)

This paper introduces KPTopic, a graph-based topic generator specifically designed for tourism research. By leveraging advanced natural language processing techniques, KPTopic extends conventional topic modeling methods through the construction of a semantic network that integrates key parts of speech, including adjectives, verbs, and nouns, to capture rich semantic relationships within textual data. The model further enhances contextual understanding by incorporating extensive noun-to-noun associations and metadata such as visit dates and business types. Applied to 11,729 TripAdvisor reviews of Finland, KPTopic produces coherent and interpretable topics that reflect tourists’ perceptions.

Academic Publications

  • Does language make a difference? Exploring the hiking experience of a nation-specific online community
    Peng Yang, Juho Pesonen
    Journal of Outdoor Recreation and Tourism (2025)

    This study explores hiking experiences of Finns within a nation-specific online community. Understanding hiking experiences is crucial for outdoor recreation and tourism management. Academic research on the topic has so far predominantly focused on English-language content and often overlooked the contextual factor of online communities. This study aims to increase our understanding of the hiking experience by comparing two groups of Finnish reviewers: those who wrote their reviews in Finnish and those who wrote in English.
